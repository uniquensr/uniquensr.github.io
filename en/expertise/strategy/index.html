Digital Strategy

Before we build anything, we work out what is worth building.

Most digital projects do not fail because the code was bad. They fail because the wrong problem was chosen at the start.

Our work begins with questions, not proposals. Where does the business actually make money? Where does the customer decide? Where do you lose them? Which part of this problem is genuinely digital, and which part is not?

The output is a document you could use without us: the problem definition, the scope, the success criteria, and the things we are deliberately not building.
