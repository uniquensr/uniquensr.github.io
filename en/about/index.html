About

Vira Noafarin is a multidisciplinary design and technology studio for websites, digital products and intelligent systems that solve real business problems.

We don't hand over projects. We take them to a result.

A digital project isn't judged on the day it ships. It's judged months later, when the numbers speak. We stay until then.

How we work

  1. 01

    Understand

    Short sessions with the people who know the work firsthand. Sales, support and operations join the conversation alongside management.

  2. 02

    Strategise

    The problem definition, the scope, the success criteria, and the list of things we are deliberately not building.

  3. 03

    Design

    From information architecture to final interface. Persian is designed first; English comes out of the same system.

  4. 04

    Build

    Development in short cycles with a viewable build. You see something every week, not one large handover at the end.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Testing, migration, training your team, and a full handover of access. Ownership is yours from day one.

  6. 06

    Grow

    We measure, learn and improve again. This stage has no fixed end date, and it matters most.

What we hold to

  • 01

    A written contract

    Scope, schedule, payment stages and the obligations of both sides are written down before work starts. Nothing begins on a verbal agreement.

  • 02

    Full ownership

    The code, database, domain, hosting and analytics accounts are in your name. If we ever stop working together, the project keeps running without us.

  • 03

    A monthly report

    After launch you receive a monthly report: what improved, what did not, and what happens next month.

  • 04

    Honesty about fit

    If a project is not a fit, or the budget does not match the scope, we say so in the first meeting. We will not take your time.
